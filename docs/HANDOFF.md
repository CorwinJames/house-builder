# House Builder: handoff brief (for Claude, and back to Grok Bot)

Last updated by Claude: 2026-11-16. Main includes PR #42 (line up selections from the Arrange bar). Plan schema is 8.

## The project
James Corwin and his wife Sophie are planning a small house built from natural materials, with hempcrete preferred. It sits on about 5 acres in Jefferson County, WA. They'll start small and add on later. Sophie does the design. James does the finish work himself: paint, flooring and tile.

This repo is a Sims-style house builder that runs in the browser:
- Repo: https://github.com/CorwinJames/house-builder
- Live (GitHub Pages, updates from `main`): https://corwinjames.github.io/house-builder/
- Main users: Sophie on a newer MacBook Pro with a trackpad, and James, sometimes on his phone.

Read the code before you change anything. Don't trust this brief on implementation details.

## Hard rules
1. Never make up a price. Only use published prices you can cite, with a source link. Anything without one stays marked "price needed".
2. Don't contact vendors or sign anything without James's approval.
3. Sophie's real plan files (`*.house.json`) never go in the repo. They live on James's Mac in `Documents/House Builder/App plans/`.
4. Do every change on a branch with a PR. James approves each merge himself.
5. Inside the builder, use plain words that teach building as you go, like a game. Trade jargon belongs only in exports and blueprints.
6. Keep the app guided. It always shows what the active tool does and what to click next, confirms each action with a short toast, and keeps a list of recent actions.

## What's built (stages 1–8 and the follow-ups)
- **Screen:** the plan and 3D fill the window under a slim top bar. New plan, Open and Save are in the File menu. The layer panel, the selection card, the view toolbars and the build dock all float on top as glass, so the drawing never resizes. The layer panel collapses with its Layers button. The Guide and Recent list open from the Guide pill in the build dock. A toast still confirms every action.
- **Build:** walls, rooms, doors and windows, in both 2D and 3D. There's one HUD that sits over the 2D view, the 3D view and Split view, plus a floating placeholder that shows the item about to be placed. 3D has Edit, Orbit and Cutaway.
- **Walls:** a wall end lands exactly where you drop it, except that it always clicks onto another wall end within about 10 pixels. Walls that touch draw as one shape: L corners miter, T joins stop at the other wall's face, in 2D and 3D. Grid snapping is opt-in, using the "Snap to grid" toggle or by holding Shift. "Tidy up shape" rounds walls to 3-inch steps and squares near-square corners. While dragging, a live label shows the length and angle (for example "12 ft 4 in at 37°"). Angles show to a tenth of a degree. 0° points east, angles go counterclockwise, and the label also shows the angle to the joined wall. Press Tab, or use the wall card, to type an exact length and angle.
- **Proportions while drawing:** Room tool options have a Shape row (Free, Square, φ, √2, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9): the dragged room keeps that ratio inside the walls. Wall tool options have "Length from the wall you start on" (Free, Same, φ, √2, 3:2, 4:3, 2:1): a wall started on a wall end is that wall × or ÷ the ratio. Snap φ, the Grid/Ratio toggle, the project Ratio and Module are gone. Tool options slide open from the Options pill, and "More options" (snap step, proportion guides) slides open inside them.
- **Selection cards:** the wall card is material first, then typed Length / Angle / Height, ends, Phase and Wiring, and a folded cost list. The room card has its name as the title, sizes, Shape chips that reshape the room by moving one wall, Fill this room, Phase, folded issues, and a folded cost list. The wall-to-wall proportion lock was taken off the card.
- **Room sizes:** taken from the actual walls, with one number per side, showing both inside and outside sizes when they differ.
- **Move and copy:** drag a door or window onto another wall. ⌘C / ⌘V copies doors, windows, scale figures, labels and site marks and pastes at the pointer (doors land on the nearest clear wall). Box-select several walls, or drag a room, to move them as one piece; a moving end clicks onto a nearby wall end.
- **Clip on or lay on:** a switch in the build dock (Lay on by default, Option flips it for one drag). Laid-on walls touch and look like one corner but move on their own. Clipped walls move as one. The toast after a drop offers the other choice, and the wall card can Unclip or Clip each end, or Detach the whole wall. With Lay on, dragging a wall, or its end handle, pulls it off its clips ("Keep it clipped" in the toast undoes that); Option-drag brings clipped walls along. Plan schema 8 stores clips in `joins`; older files treat touching ends as clipped.
- **Sense of size:** the Scale tool drops a person, queen bed, sofa, table for six or car. Walk in the 3D toolbar stands you inside at eye height. The plan shows a "You" dot while you walk; drag it to move.
- **Select:** Shift-click and box select in 2D and 3D Edit. Delete or Backspace bulk-deletes, and Undo works. The camera doesn't jump when you delete. Furniture, site marks and labels can be selected, moved and deleted.
- **Navigation:** pinch zooms toward the cursor, two-finger scroll pans, and the mouse wheel zooms. Redraws are limited to once per frame, and moving the camera doesn't rebuild the cost or code checks. The 3D camera is damped. No edit moves the camera: only New plan, Open, Import, Fit and Reset view frame the house.
- **Saving:** Google sign-in with Drive saves (Cloud project "House Builder", house-builder-514402, under jamesjamesjac@gmail.com). It autosaves a few seconds after edits, and every 30 seconds while there are unsaved changes. The Google token is kept for its hour, so a reload reopens the Drive plan with no popup. After it expires, a small "Reconnect Drive" pill in the top bar takes one click (the last account is passed as a hint). Edits made while waiting are saved to Drive on reconnect, with the newer-copy check. You can also import and export files.
- **Costs:** a live materials list and costs. Phase 1 was $63,509.72 at stage 6, with many items still "price needed".
- **Electrical:** stage 5. Many parts and the L&I electrical permit are still "price needed".
- **Plumbing:** stage 6. Pipes stay out of the hempcrete and rammed-earth exterior walls, and the drain runs to a septic stub.
- **Code rules:** stage 7, with warnings. "Show me" jumps to the item, selects it and explains the problem in a toast. 9 in of hempcrete models at about R-18, below the WSEC-R prescriptive targets, which is an open question for the designer.
- **Land:** stage 8, with a sample 5-acre lot. It covers the well, septic, grading and the legal building area. The well needs about a 100 ft setback from the drain field, and the app tries opposite corners before warning. If the lot is too tight, it suggests public water, a shared well, or a mound or pressure septic system. Well, septic and grading costs are still "price needed".
- **Sun and shade (stage 10, sunlight half):** a layer, off by default and hidden in Build unless "Keep on while building" is ticked. With a real lot it uses the lot's location, a date and a time; without one, set the sun by hand (dial, sliders, or drag the sun on the plan). The plan shows shade, wall shadows and warm sun patches through glass; 3D moves the sun, shades rooms (even in Cutaway) and adds a soft bounce in sunlit rooms.
- **Skylights:** real VELUX FS deck-mount sizes (C01 to S06), placed on the roof face over a room with a straight shaft through the attic to the ceiling. The card says how it's built (rough opening, flashing kit, shaft height, framing per IRC R802.9, 13+10 shaft insulation per WSEC-R) and what it costs. Sun and shade lets light in only when the sun faces that roof and gets down the shaft.
- **Passive solar (Solar tool):** pick the lot or a reference place; it looks up the climate, shows the noon sun through the year and a section through any window, suggests the sun-side eave, awnings per window, and roof pitch for solar collectors, and checks glass amounts and orientation. Windows have a card to set width, height, sill, top, and position along the wall exactly.
- **Clerestory and folding glass walls (from Sophie's photo):** the Roof tool's Clerestory shape makes a low front roof, a band of high glass, and a raised roof behind; set the glass height, how far back it sits, and the high roof's eave (with a suggestion). The Door card's Style row makes any door a folding glass wall.
- **Design-software arranging (for Sophie):** select one or more rooms, walls, or pieces (Shift-click, drag a box, or ⌘A) and drag the round handle to turn them, or use the Arrange bar: turn 90°, turn by any angle, flip, duplicate. R, Shift+H/V, arrow keys, and ⌘D work too. A room takes its contents and only its own stretch of shared walls; ⌘C/⌘V copy rooms and walls.
- **Templates (for Sophie):** the Templates tool lists twelve traditional buildings with small plans: from China a three-bay and a five-bay hall, a siheyuan, a sanheyuan, and a southern skywell house; from Japan a minka farmhouse, a Kyoto machiya, and a shoin house with engawa; from Korea hanok in ㅡ, ㄱ, ㄷ, and ㅁ shapes. Pick one, a see-through copy follows the pointer (R turns it, red means it would land on walls), click to place it, and it is selected as ordinary walls, rooms, doors, windows, and posts to edit. To build what these styles need, the app gained: a roof per building with four forms (hip and gable, hip, gable, flush gable), ridge direction, an eave curve, its own pitch, and a stone platform (all in the Roof tool, click a roof to pick it); a Column tool (one post or a row); a Floor height on the room card for raised floors; three wall types (timber frame with earth infill, fired brick, sliding paper screens); and jian / kan / ken bay grids in More options. New cost lines are all price needed.
- **Furnish (Sims-style):** a toilet, bathroom sink, shower, kitchen sink, stove, dishwasher, fridge, washing machine, dryer, and wardrobe at real published sizes. The piece follows the pointer, sits back against the nearest wall, and shows in plan and 3D. Pieces that need water or a 240 V circuit are also that plumbing or power point (James chose one object), so pipes, circuits, code checks, and cost follow them.
- **Pictures and tracing (for Sophie):** paste from Affinity Designer (⌘V), drop a file, or File → Add a picture…; it lies see-through over the plan, saved in the plan file. Its card sets the scale from a line of known length and turns a line drawing into walls, or walls and doors. With Affinity's "Copy items as SVG" setting on, the exact lines come across at any angle; a plain picture is traced for straight walls across and down only. A blue preview comes first; Keep adds walls, doors, and rooms.
- **Resize and groups (for Sophie):** any selection, including a picture, gets corner squares to drag bigger or smaller (proportions kept), and the Arrange bar has a Size box (150% or 40 ft). Walls stretch but keep their thickness; doors, windows, and furniture keep their real size. A traced drawing and a placed template are groups: one click selects the whole thing, a second click on a part picks just that part. Group and Ungroup are on the Arrange bar.
- **Lining up (for Sophie):** click one thing, Shift-click the others (a group, a section of rooms, a picture), and the Arrange bar's second row lines them up with the first one picked: left, centre, right, top, middle, or bottom edges. A picture is picked by clicking its dashed outline.
- **New templates (for Sophie):** Japan: Katsura shoin group, Shugakuin Jugetsukan, Shugakuin upper teahouses, Shirakawa-go gassho house; the minka now has a work doma, storage, and an inside stable. Korea: Hyangdan (Yangdong). Still to do: 仓垈里古宅 (we couldn't identify which house this is; waiting on its Korean name or place).
- **Align snap (for Sophie):** dragging a resize corner or a wall end clicks onto the edges of other rooms, walls, pictures, and furniture lined up across or up and down, or to the same width or depth as something else, with pink guide lines and the matching length shown. "Align snap" in the build dock turns it off; Ctrl/⌘ while dragging skips it once.
- **Sharper sun and shadows (for Sophie):** sun patches through windows are traced along straight lines from the sun, so the patch depends on the window's size and height, the wall's thickness (the window's sides and head cut it at an angle), and the real roof above (a high gable rake shades less than a low eave). The roof now throws its whole shadow on the ground in the plan and in 3D, where before only walls did in the plan and a flat ring stood in for the roof in 3D. Also fixed: the Sun panel failed when the page opened with the sun already on.
- **Seasons (for Sophie):** the Sun panel has Winter, Spring, Summer, and Fall buttons (Dec 21, Mar 20, Jun 21, Sep 22) that move the sun for that day at the chosen time. With no real lot, the panel asks once where the house is (a town, or lat, lon) and uses that place; the Solar tool uses the same place.
- **Speed (Nov 17):** switching Plan / Build / View and dragging went from 5–12 s to under 1 s on a busy plan. The 3D view no longer redraws 60 times a second when nothing moves, skips work while hidden, and rebuilds less often during a drag; floor area, cost, and warnings are worked out once per edit instead of on every redraw and click.
- **Plan schema 7:** furniture icons, labels, site features, and outdoor and study areas.

## Known leftovers and open questions
- Google tokens last about an hour and there is no server for a refresh token, so after an hour away Drive still needs one click. While the Cloud app is in Testing, consent can also expire after about 7 days.
- The phone layout got the same floating dock but was only checked in a phone-sized test browser, not on James's phone.
- Sophie needs to give one real measurement so the scale can be locked.
- Walls and rooms can't be copied yet. Walk mode walks through walls. Scale figures can't be turned yet.
- Dragging a room moves its walls, including walls it shares with a neighbor, which stretch the neighbor. Undo puts it back.
- A shaped room drawn against an existing wall reuses that wall, so its inside ratio can be slightly off.
- The Wall type chips list Hempcrete and Rammed earth twice (two assemblies each in data/assemblies.json share a short name). Not changed yet.
- Hempcrete R-value against code is a question for the designer.
- Skylight framing assumes rafters and ceiling joists at 24 in. on center, because the roof isn't framed yet; rafter-size lumber is "price needed" until it is. M04, S06, and the S06 flashing kit have no published price yet. Shafts are straight, not splayed. Flashing assumes shingle or slate roofing.
- The clerestory applies to the main building's roof only, uses placeholder 1 ft curb and header heights, and the sun through it ignores interior walls. Folding glass walls need a bid for price.
- Sun and shade ignores roof overhangs, trees, terrain and the second bounce of light. Lot times assume Pacific time.
- Templates don't build single-slope (shed) roofs (the skywell house's inward roofs), stepped horse-head gable walls, second storeys, or roof ornaments. Platform and raised-floor heights in the templates are placeholders, except the minka's 50 cm. Front walls of Chinese halls are drawn as solid infill with windows, not lattice. Posts are not sized.
- Tracing doesn't find curved walls or windows, and doesn't read a scale bar or dimension text: set the scale by clicking a line. Big pictures can fill the browser's storage (a toast says so); Drive still saves.
- Furnishings have no clearance zones (space in front of a toilet or washer) yet, and a fridge has no outlet point.
- Many "price needed" items are still open.

## How we hand work back and forth
1. **This file is shared memory.** Keep it at `docs/HANDOFF.md` in the repo. Whoever finishes a session updates "Last updated", the feature list and the leftovers.
2. **Add a log entry for every change** to `docs/CHANGELOG-AGENTS.md`, newest first:
   `YYYY-MM-DD | Claude or Grok Bot | PR link or commit | what changed, in plain words | anything left open`
3. **Branches and PRs.** Put each piece of work on its own branch (`claude/...` or `cursor/...`) with its own PR, and merge it to `main` only after James says yes. Don't leave half-finished work on `main`.
4. **Taking over.** Pull `main`, read this file, read the latest log entries, then run `git log --oneline -20`.
5. **Plan files.** If a change alters the `.house.json` format, bump the schema version, keep older files loading, and note it in the log.
6. **Before handing back,** make sure the live site loads with no console errors, the 2D plan isn't blank, the top-bar buttons work, and Drive save and reload still work. A blank plan and dead buttons have broken things before (fixed in PR #20).
